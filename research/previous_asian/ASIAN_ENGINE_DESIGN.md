# 32-date arithmetic Asian engine

This engine keeps the European engine's 8,192-point, stride-256 layout: two
ZMMs hold 32 paths and the 256 loop iterations cover one complete block.  The
change is that one iteration consumes all 32 Brownian-bridge dimensions for
those paths instead of pricing one terminal value.

## Modes

| Mode | Sequence construction | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `reference` | Exact OpenEvolve W table, scalar double oracle | default |
| `rank1` | D1-derived ordinary W table; at most two coefficient lines | experimental |
| `coefficient-pair` | Two dimensions share each selector plan | experimental |
| `final-z` | Transform D1 once, then five Gray-basis ZMM permutations | experimental |

Every mode is an expanding ordinary Sobol direction-number table.  The three
synthetic tables reproduce their own scalar same-sample price within `1e-4`,
but they are not claimed to reproduce OpenEvolve's projections or finance
convergence.  In particular, a matching scalar price proves implementation
correctness, not sequence quality.

## Hot data and state

There is one 2,048-slot central Gaussian coefficient bank (16 KiB for c0/c1),
not one bank per dimension.  Packed 16-bit selector states advance with XOR at
phase/block boundaries.  Two `vpermi2ps`-family operations per ZMM select the
matching coefficients.  Hard Gaussian tails are detected as a mask, evaluated
out of line, and blended back.  Generated degree-5 tail coefficients are kept
as a test asset, but the current correctness kernel uses exact Acklam fallback.

No Sobol-state matrix or path buffer is stored in L1.  The only path-state
scratch is 16 live Brownian nodes × 32 paths × 4 bytes = 2 KiB.  Leaf nodes are
priced immediately and never stored.

The final-z mode amortizes Gaussian work across the 32 dimensions, but its
permuted dimensions have poor OpenEvolve projections.  Rank1 is the conservative
hardware candidate.  Real performance must be measured on SPR; SDE timing is
emulation timing and is not a throughput result.

The checked-in asset header and manifest are generated by
`NewDirNumbers/testing/pablito_sequence/export_asian_engine_assets.py`; run it
with `--check` to detect stale tables.
